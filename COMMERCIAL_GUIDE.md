## 2. 📱 Setting Up the Telegram VIP Signal Publisher

To broadcast 1-click AI signals directly to your Telegram VIP Channel:

1. Open Telegram and search for `@BotFather`.
2. Send `/newbot`, choose a name (e.g. `SmartTradingAiBot`), and copy your **Bot Token** (e.g., `7123456789:AAE...`).
3. Create a public/private Telegram Channel and add your bot as an **Administrator**.
4. In your Smart Trading platform, click **"Коммерческие настройки"** (Commercial Settings) and paste:
   - **Telegram Bot Token**
   - **Telegram Chat/Channel ID** (e.g. `@your_vip_channel` or `-1001234567890`)
5. Now, whenever an AI signal is generated, click **"📢 Отправьте сигнал в VIP-канал Telegram"** to broadcast it to all your paying subscribers instantly!

Alternatively, run `python telegram_bot.py` on your computer/server for automated background broadcasting.
